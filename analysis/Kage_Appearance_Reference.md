# How Kage Looks

Every passage in *Tales of Being Limitless: Sticky Situation* (Prologue to S4 Ch7) that describes Kage Ozul's appearance. The first edition came from two independent sweeps: six readers going through the manuscript line by line, plus a keyword search of the whole file to catch anything they missed. This second edition re-checks every row against the revised draft. Line numbers refer to that draft. Where the revision reworded a passage, the row now quotes the new wording. Two rows were dropped because their lines are no longer in the book: Abby holding his "split knuckles" (S2 Ch6) and Zulfiya wondering why he gripped the whistle when she forfeited (S3 Ch9). One row changed speaker: the S3 Ch4 whistle clutch is now Ailani's line to Orion. The new S4 Ch7 adds no appearance details; Kage only cheers from the stands.

## What the book establishes

**Hair.** Scarlet or red. The first description is the only one that names the shade: "His arctic blue eyes stayed locked on it as his scarlet hair drooped in front of his face" (S1 Ch1, l. 20). After that, people pick him out by it ("that striking red hair," "that conspicuous red hair," "that red hair through the glass").
- **Before S2 Ch10:** It droops in front of his face. Kage later calls it "lazy," and it "usually ended up in front of my face" (l. 2202).
- **When he uses telekinesis:** It stands on end (S1 Ch10 l. 884; S1 Ch15 ll. 1244-1294; S2 Ch3 l. 1595), then droops back down (l. 1260).
- **From S2 Ch10 on:** He cuts it himself. It's "messy and layered, with long, wispy pieces framing his face. His bangs were swept up and to the side, and the top stood up so high it almost looked like it was floating. It looked downright cartoonish" (l. 2196). He wants it to look "Just like an action figure!" (l. 2202). Later it's "his big hair" (l. 3139).
- **Nicknames from the hair:**
  - "period hair" (Lucifer)
  - "red one" (Abaddon, many times)
  - "the red to my blue" (Inazuma)
  - "cherry head" (Ailani)
  - "red head" (a drunk bettor)
  - "red-head" (Rebento)

**Eyes.** Arctic blue (l. 20). He's "the blue-eyed idiot" (l. 1802). Frostbite warns "Your blue eyes are too much of a tell," so Kage wears contacts in disguise (ll. 2207-2209). **What changed in this draft:** The contacts are now yellow instead of gray, which is Abby's eye color (l. 30). Frostbite picked them, so whether or not it's meant as a tribute, Kage's disguise has him literally wearing her eyes. From the S3 Ch15 fight on, his eyes read as "almost…feral" (l. 4169).

**Face and smile.**
- **The grin:** His signature is "a crooked grin" (Prologue, l. 7; it "returned" in S1 Ch1, l. 56). Later it's "That almost iconic Kage smile" (l. 2361). Lance calls the smile "pretty comforting" (l. 4014).
- **The makeover:** In S2 Ch10 he wears eyeliner ("And is that eyeliner?!", l. 2198). It's never mentioned again.
- **The missing tooth:** Since S4 Ch4, there's "a gap in his smile" where a tooth didn't grow back (l. 4680). Lance: "Hey, you got a look now!" (l. 4688). This is the newest permanent change to his face.

**Build and height.** He's never given a number.
- **Height:** It's always relative. Orion is "seven inches taller than me" (l. 564). Lucifer looms over him and calls him "little Ozul," "pebble," and "ant." He looks up at Orion, Lucifer, Huoyan, and Anubis.
- **Build:** "You're not exactly…muscular, Kage." / "I'm just lean and not bulky like you guys" (ll. 1747-1749). Abaddon calls it his "puny body" (l. 2349).

**Skin.** Never described. Nothing in the book gives his skin tone or complexion. The Prologue only places him as "a kid in Japan" (l. 5). The only skin details are fevers, goosebumps, and the biomass moving under it.

**Nails.** His power comes out through them ("different adhesives are excreted through his nails!", l. 64). The Prologue gives him "dirty nails" (l. 9).

**Clothes and costumes.** He has no hero costume yet. Rhatella's gear "was something only the old Kage was after" (l. 2205). What he wears, in order:
- **School:** the program uniform (Prologue l. 17; S1 Ch5 l. 276).
- **First-year job, maid outfit:** "a black-and-white maid outfit, long stockings, and black heels" (photo, l. 300). Abby: "You looked so cute!"
- **First-year job, cat ears:** In the S4 Ch1 flashback he still has cat ears on from work ("They make me look so dumb!", ll. 4430-4431). Ailani remembers "his different-colored costumes" (l. 2941).
- **Barefoot:** He kicks off his shoes and socks to fight or climb (ll. 684, 1784, 1914), so glue can come from his toenails too.
- **Concrete gauntlets:** in several fights.
- **After Nerezza:** torn clothes, and Abby's scorched dolphin backpack slung over one arm (ll. 1882, 2154).
- **Frostbite's outfit:** all black with a hood (ll. 2205, 2283). "This isn't heroic in the slightest."
- **The whistle:** Abby's gold whistle goes from his pocket (l. 1487) to "a string around his neck" (l. 2225), worn under his collar (ll. 2307, 2369). He throws it to Orion before the Lucifer fight (l. 4106), and Orion gives it back in S4 Ch3 (ll. 4630-4632).
- **During the Lucifer fight:** he rips his shirt off (l. 4171).
- **Afterward:** hero course merch, because his clothes were torn (l. 4534).
- **Cheyenne's cowboy hat:** He grabs it (l. 3569), and it's the stake in Lance's bet (l. 4666).

**The biomass.**
- **Black blood:** It first shows when his bloody coughs turn "from red to black" (l. 2351).
- **Visible regrowth:** His limbs regrow while people watch:
  - "Fibers and tissues began moving under his skin, rebuilding" (l. 2375)
  - his arm heals "on camera" for the whole broadcast (ll. 4321, 4388)
- **His face:** In the smoke, black tendrils pour from his mouth and nostrils and rebuild it: "Burned tissue slagged off in slabs of skin" (l. 4169).
- **The result:** He comes out looking "untouched" or "unscathed," with "barely even scars visible" (l. 2016).
- **What changed in this draft:** Thanatos's warning is now that the mass "will amplify what's already there" (l. 2014). The last draft said "your traits, especially the negative ones." The new wording is more neutral: the biomass magnifies whoever Kage already is.

## Gaps and things to decide

1. **Skin tone, height, and build are never stated.** For a protagonist, readers will want at least one line. His height is only ever relative (Orion's own height is never given either). The S2 Ch10 makeover or an early mirror moment would be natural places for it.
2. **His hair is his telekinesis tell, but the makeover keeps it standing up.** Before S2 Ch10, hair standing on end means the power is firing. Since the makeover it always stands up ("almost looked like it was floating"). That may be deliberate, since it looks like the power is always half on. If so, one line acknowledging it would help. If not, give the telekinesis a new tell.
3. **The eyeliner** appears once (S2 Ch10) and is never mentioned again. Either keep it as part of his new look (a line at the tournament would do it) or let it go.
4. **The yellow contacts** are only for the disguise. He's back to blue eyes at the tournament (Ailani stares "deep into his blue eyes," l. 2552). That's consistent. Now that the contacts are Abby's yellow, though, it's worth deciding whether anyone notices. Frostbite chose them, so either Frostbite knows exactly what he's doing or it's a coincidence Kage could catch in the mirror. One line from either of them would turn it into a beat.
5. **He still has no hero costume.** Given the action-figure hair and Anubis's merch machine, his first costume could be a big moment.
6. **The missing tooth** (S4 Ch4) is a lasting mark from the Abaddon fight. It ties to the crooked grin, so it's worth keeping track of.
7. **Frostbite says he forgot Kage's gender** (ll. 2170, 2182). Some readers may take that as a hint that Kage looks androgynous. If you don't mean that, the joke needs a different setup.
